# Script v1: "Intelligence. Intellect."

About 2:45 at a slow documentary pace, around 380 words. Stage 1 only: no visual or sound treatment yet.

## I. What we already know (0:00-0:50)

> Intelligence.
>
> We built it.
>
> It remembers everything it has ever read. It calculates in a blink. It translates every language. Writes. Paints. Codes. Diagnoses.
>
> It never sleeps. It never tires. And every month, it gets cheaper.
>
> For the first time in history, thinking can be bought by the kilowatt.
>
> Intelligence is no longer rare. It is becoming a utility. Like electricity. Like salt.

On screen: INTELLIGENCE, then RECALL / SPEED / PATTERN / LANGUAGE / CREATION, then a cost line falling.

## II. What is missing (0:50-1:45)

> But watch what it does when no one tells it what matters.
>
> It answers. Brilliantly. Endlessly.
>
> Ask one question, and it gives you ten thousand answers. It cannot tell you which one deserves your life's work.
>
> Today's intelligence waits for a purpose.
>
> The missing piece has a name. **Intellect.**
>
> Intelligence is the power to solve. Intellect is the power to decide what is worth solving.
>
> Intelligence answers. Intellect asks.

On screen: a flood of answers, then silence, then INTELLECT (gold).

## III. Why intellect is scarce, and what sets humans apart (1:45-2:15)

> Intelligence can be copied. Intellect cannot.
>
> Intelligence scales with chips and electricity. Intellect is earned: through consequence, through loss, through years of being wrong and paying for it. You cannot download judgment.
>
> We have seen this pattern before. Other species are faster. Stronger. A dog smells what we never will. A bird crosses a continent without a map. Intelligence is everywhere in nature.
>
> What set us apart was never more of it. It was intellect: to imagine what does not exist. To ask why. To build for a future we will never see. A cathedral. A constitution. A cure.
>
> Intelligence adapts to the world. Intellect changes it.

## IV. What happens when intellect sits over intelligence (2:15-2:40)

> Now imagine the two together.
>
> Intelligence: vast, fast, tireless. And above it, intellect: choosing, judging, directing.
>
> Questions no one thought to ask finally get asked. Answers are chosen, not just generated. Speed finally has direction.
>
> Not more intelligence. Intellect sitting above it.
>
> That is the layer above.

## V. Open ending (2:40-2:55)

> But if intelligence is everywhere, and intellect is not...
>
> Who decides what it is for?
>
> A world of infinite answers, and no judgment. Unmatched power, and no direction.
>
> Intelligence will be powerful. That was never in doubt.
>
> The question is: what happens if intellect is missing?

On screen: the closing question alone, then black.
